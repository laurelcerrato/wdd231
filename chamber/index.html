Classes in Valle de Sula will be 70% face to face

The director of the National Autonomous University of Honduras in Valle de Sula (UNAH-VS), Jacobo Paredes, announced that 70 percent of classes will begin on-site, on January 23, in this regional university center. "We have been working on the face-to-face part in the area of medicine and some engineering courses last year, and at the end of the year the Higher Education Council authorized the universities to return to face-to-face classes and we hope that this first academic period will have a higher degree of face-to-face classes," he explained. The authorities expect that approximately 12,000 to 15,000 students will attend in person during this first academic period, maintaining biosecurity measures.
